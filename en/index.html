About me

A coach who races too.

I'm Sander – a triathlete and World Triathlon Level 1 Coach. I coach age-group triathletes one-to-one.

My background is in football, running and the gym. I came to triathlon at 30 – at first I just wanted to finish one race, but the sport pulled me in. Since then I've completed several middle-distance races and plenty of shorter races in Estonia. Middle distance is my favourite: a high tempo, but it takes a lot of endurance.

As a coach, I pass on what I've learned over the years. I help you reach your goals and support you so you can go into every session and race with confidence.
